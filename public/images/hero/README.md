# Image slot — `hero/`

**What goes here:** The large hero image shown in the rounded card on the right
side of the homepage hero.

**Recommended size:** `1920 × 1080` (16:9), JPG/WebP, optimised (< 400 KB).

**Suggested subject:** Clinic interior, a specialist consulting a patient, or a
confident patient portrait. Should be high-impact — it is the first image
visitors see.

**Replace:** drop your file here and update `src/data/images.ts` → `hero.main`.
